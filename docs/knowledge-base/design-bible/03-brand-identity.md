# 03 — Brand Identity

## Purpose

This document governs the identity, logotypes, brand voice, and personality indicators of Jarvis OS.

## Scope

Includes visual assets, logos, brand emblems, and contextual copy formatting rules.

## Design Goals

- **Tech-Forward Premium**: Focus on a high-fidelity visual identity matching sci-fi and developer workflows.
- **Understated Brilliance**: Avoid excessive gradients or colorful logos; let clean lines and Voltage Yellow accents represent the brand.
